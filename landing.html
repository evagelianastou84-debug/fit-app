About

What Fit is, in plain terms.

Fit is a styling and virtual try-on app. It reads body proportions from a single photo, matches them against real retailer size charts, and previews how a full outfit will actually look and fit — not a generic render.

Every recommendation links straight to the retailer's own product page, in your size, so the outfit you preview is the outfit you can buy.

We work with retailers through official affiliate and catalog partnerships — never by copying product images or data without permission. Every purchase happens on the retailer's own site.
