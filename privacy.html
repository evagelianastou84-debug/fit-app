Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Fit is an early-stage prototype. This policy describes, in plain terms, exactly what happens to your data when you use it today — not aspirational promises, but what the current code actually does.

Your photo

When you upload a photo, it is sent directly to our Fit Service for body-position analysis. The photo is processed in memory and on a temporary file that is deleted immediately after analysis completes — it is not saved to a database, not stored long-term, and not used for any purpose beyond generating your size/body-type estimate in that single request.

We do not use your photo to train any model, and we do not share it with any third party.

Body measurements and estimates

The height, estimated measurements, and body type produced from your photo exist only in your browser session (as part of the app's normal operation) and are not stored on our servers after the request completes.

Product and retailer data

Outfit suggestions come from a product catalog maintained by Fit. When you tap "Buy," you are taken to the retailer's own website via an affiliate link — Fit may earn a commission on purchases made this way, at no extra cost to you. We do not see or store your payment details; that happens entirely on the retailer's site.

What we don't do (yet)

  • We don't have user accounts or logins
  • We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising
  • We don't sell or share your data with data brokers

Hosting

The app and its backend services are hosted on Render, Supabase, and GitHub Pages. Standard hosting-provider logs (like IP address and request timestamps) may be retained briefly by these providers for security and reliability purposes, per their own policies.

Changes

As Fit grows past the prototype stage, this policy will be updated to reflect any new functionality — for example, if user accounts or persistent storage are introduced later, this page will say so clearly before that happens.
